About Project

🌿 What is Ayurveda?

Ayurveda is an ancient Indian system of medicine that focuses on maintaining physical and mental health. According to Ayurveda, every person has a unique body constitution called Dosha. The three major Doshas are:

  • Vata – Controls movement and the nervous system.
  • Pitta – Controls digestion and body heat.
  • Kapha – Controls strength, immunity, and stability.

🤖 Rule-Based Expert System

A Rule-Based Expert System is an Artificial Intelligence technique that makes decisions using predefined IF-THEN rules. This project applies Ayurvedic rules to classify the user's Dosha based on symptoms.

🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to demonstrate how traditional Ayurvedic knowledge can be integrated with Computer Science using a Rule-Based Expert System. The system helps users identify their dominant Dosha and provides basic diet and lifestyle recommendations.
